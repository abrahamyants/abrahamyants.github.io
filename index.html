More about my background +

Eight years in contracts, regulated operations, and multistate tax controversy, then two years building. At Andersen I managed audit defense, refund claims, voluntary disclosures, and nexus reviews for corporate and startup clients, and led engagements where the state tax treatment of major technology transactions was in dispute. As COO of Phenotopia I ran a $1.75 million regulated buildout through permits, construction, inspections, and opening, and took a 33-page conditional-use application through unanimous local approval and state licensing. As a judicial extern at the U.S. Department of Labor I drafted a Defense Base Act decision that was affirmed on appeal.
